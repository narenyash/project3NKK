
I am a student with a strong interest in computer science, and one of the areas I have found particularly interesting is computer vision. I became interested in computer vision because it allows computers to process and understand information from images and videos. I find it interesting because it combines programming, artificial intelligence, mathematics, and practical applications.

My interest in computer vision developed after I started learning about artificial intelligence and machine learning. Initially, I was mainly interested in how computers could learn patterns from data. When I explored image-based data, I became curious about how a computer could identify objects, classify images, and extract useful information from visual input.

I started by learning the basic concepts of image processing and machine learning. I learned about images as numerical data, preprocessing, training datasets, testing datasets, classification, and model evaluation. These concepts helped me understand that a computer does not see an image in the same way a person does. It processes numerical information and learns patterns from examples.

This made computer vision more interesting to me.

I explored convolutional neural networks because they are commonly used for image-related machine learning tasks. Learning about convolution, filters, feature extraction, pooling, and classification helped me understand how models can identify different patterns within images.

Initially, these concepts were difficult because they involved both programming and mathematical ideas. I found that implementing small examples made them easier to understand. When I could see how changing the data or model affected the output, I was able to connect the theory with practical results.

I have worked on different projects involving computer vision and image-based machine learning. These projects gave me experience with the basic workflow of an AI application.

The first step is usually collecting or obtaining suitable data.

The next step is preparing the data.

Images may need to be resized, normalised, or converted into a suitable format. The dataset may also need to be divided into training, validation, and testing sets.

After that, the model can be trained and evaluated.

I learned that the quality of the dataset can have a major effect on the final result. If the data is too limited or does not represent the conditions in which the system will be used, the model may not perform reliably.

This was one of the most important lessons I learned from computer vision projects.

A model can produce good results in a controlled environment but behave differently when the input changes.

For example, changes in lighting, image quality, background, camera angle, or object position can affect performance.

This made me understand why testing is important.

I became interested in improving models not simply by changing the architecture but also by improving the data and evaluation process.

I also learned about data augmentation. Techniques such as rotation, cropping, flipping, and other transformations can help create more varied training examples. These methods helped me understand how models can be prepared for different types of input.

Another important part of my learning was understanding evaluation.

A model should not be judged only by how well it performs on the training data. It needs to be tested on data that it has not seen before.

This helped me understand concepts such as overfitting and generalisation.

A model that memorises the training examples may appear successful but may not work well on new images.

This showed me why proper evaluation is important in machine learning.
