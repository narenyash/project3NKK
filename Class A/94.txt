One of the first concepts I became interested in was sensors.

Robots need information about their environment.

Different sensors can provide different types of information.

Cameras can provide visual information.

Distance sensors can measure how far an object is.

Temperature sensors can measure environmental conditions.

Motion sensors can detect movement.

Other sensors can measure orientation, acceleration, or pressure.

The robot can use this information to understand what is happening around it.

I found this interesting because sensors act as a connection between the physical world and software.

A robot cannot make a useful decision if it does not have enough information about its environment.

This made me interested in sensor fusion.

A single sensor may not provide a complete picture.

For example, a camera can provide detailed visual information, but it may have difficulty in poor lighting.

A distance sensor can provide useful measurements but may not identify what an object actually is.

Combining information from multiple sensors can provide a more reliable understanding of the environment.

I want to learn more about how different sensor inputs can be combined.

Another area I find interesting is computer vision for robotics.
