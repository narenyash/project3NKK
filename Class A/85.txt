Robotics introduced me to this possibility.

A robot can use sensors to understand its surroundings, software to process information, and motors or other components to perform actions. This creates a complete system where software can interact with the physical world.

I find this combination very interesting.

One of the first concepts that attracted my attention was robotic perception. A robot needs information about its environment before it can make decisions. Sensors can provide information about distance, movement, light, temperature, sound, or images.

Cameras are particularly interesting to me because they allow robots to use computer vision.

A camera can capture an image.

A computer vision model can analyse it.

The system can identify objects or other visual information.

The robot can then use that information to decide what to do.

This connects my interest in computer vision with robotics.

Another area I find interesting is navigation.

A robot operating in an unfamiliar environment needs to determine where it is and where it needs to go. It may need to avoid obstacles, understand its surroundings, and choose an appropriate path.

This is more difficult than simply moving from one fixed point to another.

The environment may change.

People may move around the robot.

Objects may appear in unexpected locations.

The robot therefore needs to continuously process information.

I find this type of problem interesting because it combines perception, algorithms, and decision-making.

Path planning is another concept I want to understand better.

A robot may have several possible paths to a destination.

The best path may depend on distance, obstacles, time, energy consumption, or safety.

Algorithms can be used to find suitable paths.

This connects robotics with algorithms and optimisation, which are important areas of computer science.

I want to study these concepts more deeply.

Another area that interests me is autonomous robots.

An autonomous robot should be able to perform certain tasks without requiring continuous human control.

This requires several components to work together.

The robot needs sensors.

It needs software.

It needs a decision-making system.

It needs a method of controlling its physical movement.

It also needs to handle unexpected situations.

I find the engineering challenge of connecting all these components interesting.

Artificial intelligence can play an important role in autonomous systems.

I find this distinction important.

Using AI simply because it is available does not always make a system better.

The technology should be selected according to the problem.

This is an approach I want to follow in my own projects.
