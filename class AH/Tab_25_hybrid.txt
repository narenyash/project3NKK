In twilight's gentle embrace, my rescue Plott Hound, Finn, becomes an insistent, nocturnal visitor, nudging open the door to my sanctuary every five minutes after sunset, his eyes reflecting a persistent eagerness that I can't help but respond to. "Dear Finn, my loyal companion, your name lingers in the recesses of my memory, a beacon that illuminates the path of my past. Over the years, our shared journey through life's labyrinthine corridors has etched an indelible mark upon me – one that I carry with me as I stand at the threshold of a new adventure. Though we may have drifted apart, your presence remains a potent reminder of the camaraderie and growth forged during our time together." Dashing down the stairs in a flurry, he seizes the leash with eagerness, his tail wagging expectantly beside him as he stands poised by the front door, eyes gleaming with anticipation for our daily adventure together. I look forward to our walks each night. Emerging beneath the inky velvet of twilight's cloak, I am enveloped by an invigorating tranquility that soothes my soul. Each breath fills my lungs with the crisp, refreshing aroma of night-blooming flowers, while the symphony of crickets and nocturnal creatures harmonizes in a rhythmic lullaby that echoes through the silence. In these moments, I find solace from the chaos of everyday life, and I am reminded of my insatiable curiosity to explore the world and unravel its mysteries. Walking through the stillness of the sleeping town is meditative for me. It allows me to find balance amid the mental and physical toll of my other endeavors: school, filmmaking, and ice hockey. Whether I’m studying for Econ, need a break from a video editing deadline, or just back from a grueling practice, taking Finn on a walk serves as a much-needed mental reset. The contrast between suicide sprints on the ice and the tranquility of the walk helps me find balance midst life’s hectic pace. During our walk, I periodically stop to let him sniff his surroundings. Being a hound, Finn is constantly looking for a new scent or sometimes an old favorite. While Finn processes the world through his nose, I find things visually that I haven’t noticed before: the long line of ants marching in unison or the flash of a skunk’s black and white tail. Engaging in captivating narratives, I often recount the origin stories of the enigmatic beings whose paths have intersected with mine. These tales reveal not only their unique characteristics but also shed light on the intricate tapestry of events that led to these encounters, weaving together a rich narrative of shared experiences and transformative moments. Embarking on a quest reminiscent of Finn's odyssey through foreign landscapes in pursuit of novel scents, my own journey mirrors his relentless pursuit - I seek out fresh viewpoints. This insatiable curiosity has led me to immerse myself in diverse cultures, engage in stimulating discussions with intellectuals from various backgrounds, and delve deep into the pages of thought-provoking literature. Each new perspective gleaned is like a scent that lingers on my mind, enriching my understanding of the world and fueling my insatiable appetite for knowledge. Over the summer, I worked alongside members of the Mira Costa Cinema Club to create a narrative short film “12:48,” a movie about grief, misunderstanding, and the main character’s perceived role in his friend’s death due to a car accident. Serving as the principal scribe for our cinematic production, I convened a series of narrative conferences, where we delved into meticulous discussions regarding the fluidity of dialogues, seamless segues between scenes, and the unbroken chain of narrative consistency. As I neared the final strokes in crafting my narrative, the insights and perspectives of my fellow club members would invariably surface, frequently offering a stark contrast to my initial vision. Their unique interpretations not only challenged me to reconsider certain aspects of my story but also sparked a profound sense of collaboration and shared creativity that transformed the act of writing into a truly collaborative endeavor. A member of our group had recently lost their grandmother; based on her comments I realized the denial aspect of grief was missing from the dialogue. Amidst a flurry of revisions, I meticulously crafted a pivotal scene in my narrative, delving deep into the psyche of the protagonist, Mac. This scene captures Mac's valiant yet futile attempt to maintain a semblance of his routine life, as he grapples with an overwhelming wave of guilt that threatens to engulf him. In this poignant tableau, Mac's steely resolve crumbles under the relentless burden of self-recrimination, offering a glimpse into his emotional turmoil and the depth of his character. My best work comes from collaborating with people who bring varied perspectives to a screenplay, creating a sharper, more multidimensional story. If Finn has his way, we’d be out all night, sniffing every single blade of grass. In his insatiable quest for knowledge, my father's restless spirit consistently inspires me. His relentless pursuit of novelty and discovery has carved a path that I am eager to follow, as I too yearn to push the boundaries of understanding and explore the vast expanse of human potential. The spark that ignites in him when he uncovers a hidden truth or solves a complex puzzle is a flame that burns brightly within me. His passion for exploration and learning has not only fueled my intellectual curiosity but also shaped my aspirations, making me determined to carve out my own niche in the realm of academic discovery. I often feel the same way. Like Finn, I’m always sniffing out my next business idea. Much like following a scent, good ideas require me to keep my nose to the ground. Embracing each prospective opportunity with the tenacity of a seasoned surfer, I find myself perpetually poised and prepared to ride the crest of life's ever-changing waves. These moments are not mere experiences to be passively encountered; they are challenges to conquer, lessons to learn, and triumphs to celebrate.
