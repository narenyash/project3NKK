THE “MOMENTS WHERE THE SECONDS STAND STILL” COLLEGE ESSAY EXAMPLE
Montage Essay, “Other/Advanced” type
I hold onto my time as dearly as my Scottish granny holds onto her money. I’m careful about how I spend it and fearful of wasting it. Precious minutes can show someone I care and can mean the difference between accomplishing a goal or being too late to even start and my life depends on carefully budgeting my time for studying, practicing with my show choir, and hanging out with my friends. However, there are moments where the seconds stand still. It is already dark when I park in my driveway after a long day at school and rehearsals. In a symphony of enthusiasm, my dog Kona greets me each day with an exuberant dance that leaves no tile untouched. As the door swings open, Kona leaps into action, propelling herself across the floor in a joyful blur, tail wagging furiously as she prepares to welcome me home with boundless affection. I run with him into my parent’s bedroom, where my mom, dad, and sister are waiting for me. We pile onto my parents’ bed to talk about what’s going on in our lives, plan our next trip to the beach, tell jokes, and “spill tea.” In my journey thus far, I've learned to perceive hurdles not as insurmountable obstacles but as stepping stones that foster growth. This pragmatic outlook, honed through experiences both personal and academic, anchors me firmly in the pursuit of what truly matters. Immersed in the whirlwind of daily activities, I seize a fleeting respite, momentarily suspending the ticking clocks that govern my bustling existence. In this transient lull, I find solace, drifting away from the relentless pace that characterizes my life as a student and an athlete. In the vibrant confines of our show choir sanctuary, a harmonious symphony of mirth erupts as we exchange witty banter and spontaneous dance moves, the air resonating with our collective camaraderie and infectious enthusiasm. Our shared moments of levity serve as a testament to the rich tapestry of connections forged within this musical family. Enveloped in a haze of fatigue, we scarcely notice as the clock ticks past the third hour of our relentless rehearsal. Unwavering dedication to our craft propels us deeper into the performance, oblivious to the passage of time.
