Nestled within the heart of a humble Nigerian village, I was born into a world defined by economic hardship. My grandfather, an indomitable spirit, instilled in me the importance of education, whispering fervently, "I traversed eight miles each day to reach the solitary school in our village." His words have resonated within me since childhood, serving as a potent reminder of the selfless acts that paved our family's path.

Born into a life where opportunities were scarce, my grandfather perceived education as the sole means of escape. Against seemingly insurmountable obstacles, he secured a coveted spot at Columbia University, transforming his existence and establishing a legacy for generations to come. Though he has since departed from this world, his tales continue to resonate within me, providing a beacon to guide my own journey.

In the crucible of pre-Civil Rights America, my father courageously waged a relentless fight against deeply entrenched racial prejudice. Seeking solace and academic nourishment, he found sanctuary within the venerable halls of Columbia University, a bastion where his prodigious intellect could flourish amidst the formidable challenges that lay beyond its protective boundaries. With a wry, biting humor, he would often recount, "I broke my knees running away from white women," a poignant, albeit disquieting, acknowledgement of the pervasive paranoia and fear that many Black men faced during that tumultuous era. Yet, in the face of such adversity, he remained undeterred, forging an illustrious career as a distinguished medical professional, thereby underscoring the unquestionable worthiness of his father's monumental sacrifices.

Hailing from American soil, I've been granted prospects that once seemed unattainable for my grandfather and father. Their tenacious struggles, however, remain ever-present within me, serving as the unyielding motivation driving my pursuit to extend their legacy. Columbia University transcends being merely an institution; it represents the bedrock of our family's dreams and the furnace that tempered our indomitable spirit. To me, Columbia stands as a solitary beacon, a place where no other option compares—its rich history is interlaced with my own, creating a unique environment where my aspirations can flourish unabated. The prospect of being the third generation of Columbia graduates and medical professionals in our lineage is not merely an ambition; it is a sacred obligation I carry within me. To forge this connection to my ancestors and contribute to the esteemed legacy of Columbia is a life mission I am eager to embark on.

Every exam and extracurricular is a step I take toward my future. The culmination of these efforts is a tribute to the miles my grandfather crawled and the battles my father fought—literally. Columbia is not just my dream—it is my inheritance, my destiny, and my promise to them.

I've made my choice.

Now make yours.
