THE “FOOD” COLLEGE ESSAY EXAMPLE
Montage Essay, “I Love/I Know” Type
I’ve spent most of my life as an anti-vegetable carboholic. In the culinary domain of my childhood home, for eons, industrially-produced snacks reigned supreme, casting a long shadow over humble, plant-based alternatives. Animal proteins were the undisputed rulers, while fruits and vegetables languished in obscurity.

But that kingdom began to crumble when I embarked on my personal journey towards healthier living, inspired by a deepening appreciation for the environment and its creatures. I began to question the impact of my dietary choices, realizing they extended far beyond the confines of our home kitchen. This awakening marked the beginning of a transformation in both my culinary tastes and ethical values.

Today, the landscape of my kitchen has been irrevocably altered. The once-dominant processed snacks have been usurped by vibrant fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and plant-based proteins. This transition has not only improved my physical wellbeing but also expanded my culinary horizons, allowing me to savor the flavors of a more sustainable and compassionate lifestyle. As I stand at the helm of this newfound kitchen kingdom, I am proud to lead by example, advocating for a healthier, greener world through my choices at the table. My transformation began with my mom’s cancer diagnosis. My mom went on a 100% whole food plant-based diet. I fully embraced this new eating philosophy to show my support. Intrigued by the burgeoning vegan movement, my partner and I embarked on a captivating journey through the realms of health documentaries, immersing ourselves in thought-provoking films like "What the Health" and "Forks Over Knives". These riveting exposés ignited within us a desire to decipher the complexities of adopting a plant-based lifestyle, fueling our passion for understanding its profound implications on our wellbeing. We read all the books by the featured doctors like “The China Study” and “How Not To Die”. My fascination with nutritional science deepened as I delved into the intricate ways specific foods can harness the power to combat cancer or stimulate metabolic processes, transforming my perspective on the role of diet in maintaining health and combating disease.

This captivating exploration not only enlightened me about the potential for dietary intervention but also sparked a profound curiosity within me to unravel the complexities hidden within this dynamic field. I found myself enthralled by the idea that the food we consume can serve as both a preventative measure and a powerful tool in our arsenal against disease, propelling my desire to delve deeper into this captivating realm. Each new food I discovered gave me an education on the role diet plays on health. I learned that, by eating sweet potatoes and brown rice, you could cure acne and heart disease. I discovered eating leafy greens with citrus fruits could boost iron absorption rates. In the realm of culinary artistry, I found an unexpected passion: crafting harmonious meals that strike a perfect balance between macronutrients. Each dish I prepared was not just about satiating hunger, but rather, it was a symphony of proteins, carbohydrates, and fats orchestrated to promote health and well-being. The joy I derive from this pursuit is profound; it's the thrill of a scientist experimenting in a kitchen lab, where each recipe is a testament to my dedication and the ultimate expression of my commitment to holistic living. Unveiling a little-known nutritional secret, the harmonious union of beans and rice creates a comprehensive protein source that has been sustaining countless populations around the world for centuries. In the heart of my kitchen, this symbiotic duo not only nourishes my body but also embodies the spirit of resourcefulness and adaptability that I strive to embody in all aspects of my life. The beans, tender yet robust, are a testament to resilience, much like the challenges I have faced and persevered through during my academic journey. Simultaneously, the rice, soft and versatile, mirrors my ability to adapt and evolve with new ideas and perspectives. This culinary anecdote is more than just a meal; it encapsulates my commitment to understanding the interconnectedness of seemingly disparate elements and harnessing their synergy for personal growth and global impact. Nourishment has transformatively cultivated within me an unwavering passion for sustainability. The realization that every morsel I consume impacts the environment and its inhabitants has instilled in me a deep-seated commitment to responsible consumption. Through meticulous research, volunteer work at community gardens, and thoughtful meal planning, I have endeavored to lessen my ecological footprint while promoting awareness of food's impact on our planet. My journey toward sustainable living is not simply about reducing waste; it's a lifestyle choice that embodies the principles of empathy, mindfulness, and interconnectedness - values that I bring to every facet of my life, including academics and extracurricular activities. Living plant-based also saves the planet from the impact of animal agriculture. For the same amount of land space, a farmer can produce 200 kilograms of soybeans versus 16 kilograms of beef. I do my part to have as small of an ecological footprint as I can. I stopped using plastic snack bags and instead turned to reusable beeswax wraps. My favorite reusable appliance is my foldable straw. If I am going to nourish my body, shouldn’t I also want to nourish the earth? My journey toward healthy living led me to becoming co-leader of the Northern Nevada PlantPure Pod, “Biggest Little Plant Pod”, a group dedicated to spreading the message about the whole food plant-based lifestyle. We are currently working on a restaurant campaign to encourage local eateries to create a plant-based, oil-free menu option and become PlantPure certified. Nurturing a growing awareness of the widespread reliance on oils in culinary practices, I found myself compelled to bring about a change – a shift towards a healthier, more sustainable food landscape. This transformation began with the conception of an oil-free, plant-based cafe, a beacon of nourishment untainted by traditional cooking methods.

In a world where oil is ubiquitous in restaurant kitchens, my vision is to carve out a space that champions the underdog – the humble plants, capable of delivering flavor and sustenance without the need for animal fats or harmful oils. This endeavor embodies not just a business venture, but a passionate crusade to bridge the gap between health-conscious consumers and the dining establishments that cater to them.

By creating an oil-free haven where plant-based cuisine thrives, I aim to challenge the status quo and inspire others to adopt healthier eating habits. My cafe will serve as a living testament to the notion that delicious food can be both nutritious and sustainable – a harmonious fusion of flavor, wellbeing, and environmental responsibility. This endeavor represents more than just another business venture; it is a chance to lead by example, proving that compassionate choices can pave the way for a brighter future in dining. My dream is to open up my very own affordable oatmeal cafe based on my Instagram page, morning_mOATivations. And I know that oatmeal isn’t the sexiest superfood out there, so here’s my sales pitch: I’m going to make oatmeal the Beyonce of the breakfast world- sweet, sassy, and power packed. This allows me to educate people about nutritional science through the stomach. Finally, I am a strong proponent of hands-on experience for learning what good food looks and tastes like, so cooking is one of my favorite ways to teach the benefits of a plant-based lifestyle. Engaging my palate as an impromptu culinary classroom, I've uncovered the harmonious and discordant dance of flavors. This tactile education method has proven efficacious, as I find that knowledge is more indelibly etched when it intertwines with personal sensory experiences of savoring nutritious, delectable meals. In our contemporary world, a pervasive narrative persists that indulging in delectable cuisine equates to guilt-ridden indulgence. However, this belief fails to acknowledge the myriad ways in which nourishing and savory meals can enrich our lives beyond mere physical sustenance.

A visit to my grandmother's farmhouse during summers has been instrumental in my understanding of this concept. The ripe tomatoes plucked straight from the vine, the sweet, earthy beets freshly harvested, and the zesty lemons hanging from the trees - each bite was a testament to nature's bounty and an ode to the joys of simple, honest cooking. My grandmother, with her deft hands and boundless passion for food, transformed these humble ingredients into meals that nourished not only our bodies but also our spirits. Through her teachings, I learned to appreciate the artistry in preparing a meal and the communal bonding it fosters.

This appreciation was further reinforced when I spent a summer interning at a farm-to-table restaurant. Watching the chefs skillfully craft dishes from fresh, locally-sourced ingredients instilled in me an understanding of food as not just a source of sustenance but also as a medium for artistic expression. The meals we served were a celebration of seasonality and locality, and they inspired in our patrons a reverence for the culinary craft that extended far beyond the confines of their plates.

My love for food extends beyond consumption and preparation; it is a deep-rooted passion that drives me to explore its historical, cultural, and social dimensions. Through my academic pursuits, I aim to delve deeper into this fascination, and ultimately use my knowledge to advocate for a paradigm shift in the way society perceives food and nourishment. A world where delectable meals are celebrated rather than feared, and where the act of sharing a meal fosters connection and community, is one I am eager to help bring into existence. Immerse oneself in the ecstasy of discovering a culinary creation so enchanting that it stirs the soul, only to find that this symphony for the senses also delivers an orchestra of health advantages to the body. Such was my experience with a particular dish, whose allure I could not resist and whose virtues I have since devoted myself to uncovering. Amidst the collective groans of fatigue that permeate the halls of my high school, I find myself brimming with an invigorating vitality. This transformation in my energy levels can be attributed to a diligent overhaul of my diet, focusing on optimizing macronutrients, essential vitamins, and minerals. No longer do I succumb to the exhaustion that plagues my peers; instead, I have found a renewed zest for life that propels me forward each day. In this capacity, I have consistently challenged my physical limits, ultimately flourishing as a distance runner and securing the title of Most Improved athlete on my high school's esteemed Cross Country team. Despite my maturation into adulthood, I remain unapologetically discerning when it comes to my palate. As a child, my parents would often lament the limited repertoire of dishes that graced my plate, but now as a prospective college student, I view this quirk not merely as a nuisance, but as an opportunity for self-discovery and personal growth. Over the years, I have learned to navigate culinary landscapes with strategic precision, seeking out establishments that cater to my particular preferences while also expanding my horizons by sampling new flavors in manageable portions. This journey of gastronomic exploration has instilled in me an appreciation for diversity and a knack for adaptability, qualities that I believe will serve me well as I navigate the rich and varied academic offerings at your esteemed institution. In my culinary journey, a transformation has unfolded. Once upon a time, my palate was as unrefined as an untouched canvas, craving simplicity in every bite. However, my taste buds have since matured, evolving beyond the confines of basic flavors to seek out complexity and nuance. I now relish the opportunity to savor exotic spices, handcrafted artisanal cheeses, and local farm-to-table produce, experiencing a world of culinary delights that once remained unexplored. This transformation in my gastronomic preferences mirrors my academic growth - I've transitioned from merely surviving academically to eagerly embracing intellectual challenges, eager to explore new ideas and engage in thoughtful discourse. This evolving appreciation for the intricate and the extraordinary is a testament to my personal development, and I am excited to bring this passion and hunger for knowledge to your esteemed institution. Rather than a carboholic, I choose to call myself a vegeholic.
