Montage Essay, “Home” Type
Before I came to America, I drank Puer Tea with my father every morning in my bedroom, sitting cross-legged on Suzhou-silk mats beside a view of the Lakeside reservoir. Nestled alongside an antique mahogany sideboard, our fingertips traced the intricate patterns on delicate porcelain teacups, as the soothing fragrance of brewing tea wafted through the air, enveloping us in a warm embrace.

(Original source: Common App Prompts) As we faced the French window, my father would share the news he read in China Daily: the Syrian civil war, climate change, and gender equality in Hollywood. Most of the time, I only listened. Intrigue pulsed through me with every shard of information that unfurled before my eyes. Each tidbit served to fuel my insatiable hunger for knowledge, igniting within me a fervor to delve deeper into the unknown. My mind, a restless tide perpetually in search of new horizons, found itself drawn towards these captivating narratives like a moth to a flame. Intriguingly, a private resolve formed within me – a determination to share the day's headlines not as mere reportage, but through the prism of my unique viewpoint.

I yearned for a moment to bridge the gap between our worlds, to translate the complexities of the news into language that would resonate with him. So, every evening, I would meticulously curate the day's events, selecting stories that not only captured his interest but also provided opportunities to engage in meaningful conversations about the world around us. This became my secret mission – a quest to forge connections and foster understanding, one news story at a time. So, I decided to study in America to learn more about the world. Embarking on an intensive, year-long exploration, I journeyed across continents and cultures, culminating in my relocation to the United States for ninth grade. Nestled within the embrace of a welcoming host family, I found not just shelter, but a sanctuary that fostered my growth and adaptation to this new environment. Nestled within the labyrinthine halls of my dormitory, my new room bore an eerie silence that resonated with the quietude of a library, devoid of the warmth and camaraderie that I had grown accustomed to in my childhood home. The walls, stark and bare, echoed with the absence of the countless tales and laughter that once filled its corners. The room felt like an empty canvas, waiting for the vibrant strokes of life and experiences yet to come, but alas, it was devoid of the comforting aroma of brewing tea and the cacophony of animated conversations that had been a constant companion throughout my formative years. However, this vacuum served as a poignant reminder of the transformative journey ahead, and I eagerly embraced it as an opportunity to weave new stories within these vacant walls. Nestled within my daily commute home from church, I stumbled upon the inviting haven of Blue House Cafe, which swiftly became an integral study sanctuary for me. The cozy ambiance, punctuated by the aroma of freshly brewed coffee and the soft hum of intellectual discourse, offered a comforting contrast to the sterile environment of my school library, fostering an atmosphere conducive to focused and productive learning. Here, amidst the eclectic blend of patrons ranging from students engrossed in textbooks to retirees sipping on steaming lattes, I felt a sense of camaraderie that spurred my own academic growth. The diverse setting not only enriched my intellectual pursuits but also exposed me to varied perspectives and ideas, thereby broadening my horizons beyond the confines of the classroom. With white walls, comfortable sofas, and high stools, Blue House is spacious and bright. Hearing people’s stories and looking at their warm smiles when they taste various pastries as I sat by the window, I watched as a production designer scouted locations for his film, or a painter took notes while brainstorming for his freehand brushwork of Blue House. Nestled in the cozy embrace of an early morning, I immerse myself in a steaming mug of coffee as I delve into the intricate labyrinth of differential and parametric equations, meticulously preparing for my forthcoming AP Calculus examination. Simultaneously, I absorb the subtleties of eloquent oratory by absorbing the wisdom imparted in Michael Sandel's Justice lectures on my laptop screen, honing my skills as a budding public speaker. In parallel, I engage in strategic planning for charity events, ensuring my role as a catalyst for positive change within my non-profit organization. Each day unfolds as a symphony of intellectual challenge, personal growth, and compassionate action. I’ve also learned by watching leaders host meetings at the rectangle conference table at the back of the cafe In intriguing symposiums, I keenly absorb insights from the captains steering the discourse, poised at the table's edge with a firm grip. Their articulate expressions of innovative thoughts continue to serve as my guiding beacon in intellectual growth. As President of the vibrant and diverse International Students Club, I took it upon myself to foster a sense of unity and camaraderie among our members. To achieve this, I would regularly convene meetings over steaming cups of coffee at a quaint local café, inviting my dedicated teammates to join me in open discussions. These gatherings were more than just academic discussions; they were cultural exchange sessions where students shared stories, traditions, and experiences that transcended the boundaries of language and geography. The aroma of freshly brewed coffee would fill the air as we engaged in heartfelt conversations about our shared dreams, fears, and aspirations for the future. These intimate gatherings not only strengthened the bonds between us but also provided an opportunity for everyone to learn from one another's rich cultural backgrounds. Coordinating the schedule with other members in Blue House has become a frequent event. Consuming several cups of coffee, my team and I have planned Lunar New Year events, field trip to the Golden Gate Bridge, and Chinese lunch in school to help international students feel more at home. Amidst the hushed reverberations of a boardroom, I straightened my spine, squared my shoulders, and with an ardor that could ignite a dormant ember, I rose to my feet behind the polished mahogany table. My eyes flashed with conviction as I passionately articulated my creative vision, captivating the room's attention. Following every encounter, we savored the rich, buttery aroma of homemade buttermilk coffee cake that lingered between us like a comforting reminder of our connection. Its warm, golden slices seemed to symbolize the unspoken bonds forged through our shared experiences and camaraderie. In my spot next to the window, I also witnessed different kinds of people. I viewed visitors dragging their luggage, women carrying shopping bags, and people wandering in tattered clothes –the diversity of San Francisco. In the heart of the city, two summers past, a vibrant spectacle unfolded before my eyes as volunteers, adorned in bold City Impact apparel, distributed sandwiches and steaming cups of hot cocoa to the homeless congregating beyond a bustling café's doors.

The scene was one of stark contrasts: the cheerful volunteers, brimming with compassion, against the backdrop of those less fortunate, their lives a harsh reminder of society's invisible cracks. Yet, amidst the cold reality, there was warmth in the shared moment, as humanity transcended temporary barriers and found solace in the simple act of offering comfort to one another. That encounter left an indelible mark on me, fostering a sense of empathy and social responsibility that I carry with me today. I investigated more about City Impact and eventually signed up to volunteer. No longer was I a bystander. At holiday outreach events, I prepared and delivered food to homeless people. While sharing my coffee, I listened to a story from an older Chinese man who told me, in Mandarin, how he had been abandoned by his children and felt lonely. Last summer, I returned to Xiamen, China, and taught my father how to drink coffee. Now, a Chemex and teapot are both on the end table. Instead of simply listening, I shared my experiences as a club president, a community leader, and a volunteer. In a buzzing workshop, I unveiled my meticulously crafted business strategy to the seasoned entrepreneur, his eyes scanning each line with an intensity that mirrored the passion I'd poured into its creation. The room fell silent as I revealed my cutting-edge prototypes, each one embodying the ingenuity and determination that had driven me throughout this ambitious venture. His evaluation was a moment of anticipation, a testament to the culmination of countless hours spent brainstorming, iterating, and refining my vision into a tangible reality. In the soft glow of our kitchen, my father lifted his steaming mug of coffee, his eyes shining with pride as he toasted, "To you, my daughter!" The heartfelt words resonated in the quiet room, marking a poignant moment that underscored the countless hours I've spent studying and striving for success. His toast served as a testament to the unwavering support that has fueled my academic journey. I am so proud of you.” Then, he patted my head as before. Together, we emptied our cups while the smell of coffee lingered.
