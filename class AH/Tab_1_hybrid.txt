The “My Pillows & Me” Example College Essay
“I can’t sleep without them!” my face scrunched up, clutching piles of cushions in an uncharacteristic moment of seven-year-old stubbornness. An overnight trip without my Stitch face pillow? My dolphin pillow pets? This was absolutely unacceptable, robbing me of the comfort and structure that was my nightly routine. Nestled within the sanctuary of my bedroom, a decade has passed since I first sank into the embrace of my bespoke assortment of pillows – a testament to the multifaceted tapestry of my identity. Each pillow holds a unique facet of my persona, cradling memories as diverse as the hues that color them: one plush and comforting, reminding me of the tender-hearted empath I strive to be; another firm yet inviting, echoing the resilient determination that propels my every step; a third soft and whimsical, capturing the imaginative dreamer within; and finally, a fourth emblazoned with inspirational quotes that spur me onwards towards my aspirations. As I recline upon these symbols of selfhood, I am reminded of the transformation that has unfolded over these years – the growth, the challenges, the triumphs, all encapsulated within the cushioned confines of my cherished collection. Nestled by my side, my cherished chibi avocado pillow stands as a testament to my adventurous culinary journey – a token of celebration bestowed upon me by my parents after I ventured into the realm of guacamole for the first time. Green, mushy, yucky! I once wrinkled my nose in disgust at the outward appearance of this vegetable (fruit?): Before the tantalizing aroma of freshly mashed avocados wafted through the air at our cherished local Mexican eatery, a sanctuary where families gather and laughter resounds, I would eagerly take my seat, savoring the anticipation as plates were prepared table-side. Each morsel of guacamole, meticulously crafted by the skillful hands of the chef, was a testament to the rich culinary heritage that unites our community and fuels my passion for exploration and connection with diverse cultures. Nestled within the plush cushions of this pillow now, I find myself both chuckling at the memory of the "avocado incident" and reflecting poignantly upon the wisdom imparted by my parents. That seemingly ordinary avocado had served as a potent symbol, urging me to reconsider the value hidden within opportunities that initially appear unpromising.

This recollection reminds me not only of the whimsical humor that punctuated our family life but also of the profound lesson it conveyed - never to shy away from seizing new possibilities based solely on their initial appearance. The memory, like this pillow, remains a cherished testament to my upbringing and serves as an enduring inspiration for my future endeavors. Nestled beside the ripe avocado, a sumptuous teal cushion from Target, its vibrant feathers artfully ruffled, perches atop a luxuriant duvet of the same hue. The pillow's soft plushness promises solace, while the duvet's elegant design exudes an air of sophistication that seamlessly blends modern and classic aesthetics. Enthralled by a burst of creativity, I purchased a unique pillow during my middle school years, inspired by an artistic piece showcased in the spring edition of American Girl Magazine. The intricate pattern and vibrant colors caught my eye, igniting a spark that transformed my bedroom into a personal sanctuary of self-expression. Design continues to fuel my burning creative spark, whether through computing or character art. In the enlightening world of coding unveiled by "Coding Projects in Scratch," I found a spark that ignited my passion for programming, and from this ember emerged a creative blaze of my own design. I crafted intricate graphics-based puzzles and engrossing platformers, each a testament to the fundamental principles of computer science I was eager to explore further. This introduction to block-based coding served as a springboard, propelling me into higher realms of programming proficiency in Python and Java – languages that now form the cornerstone of my academic pursuits. Immersed in the kaleidoscopic world of anime, I've devoted countless hours delving into Christopher Hart's comprehensive "Master Guide to Drawing Anime." With each page turn, I unearth the secrets of dynamic character composition and the intricate artistry of cell shading, techniques that have since transformed my digital art. No longer are my creations mere static images; they now pulse with life and vibrancy, a testament to the transformative power of this artistic journey. Propped against the headboard, a My Melody pillow cushions my head, a nod to my former aversion to all things “cute.” At age thirteen, the mere thought of being seen sleeping with a pink, fluffy, bunny pillow (Hello Kitty-branded, no less!) horrified me. Immerse myself I do, in the world of My Melody, a realm where I gleefully indulge my whimsical nature. Here, I lose myself to the infectious rhythms of bubbly idol music, dancing with abandon and embracing the vibrant energy that fills the air. The virtual popstar Hatsune Miku, with her twin tails shimmering turquoise, becomes an emblem of my joyous spirit, eliciting squeals of delight as I revel in her lively presence. While tuning synthesized singing vocals for my VOCALOID cover channel on YouTube, my inner geek shines through my usually studious and composed exterior. Lounging lazily beside my bed sits the master pillow, Slothogus, the 3 foot tall, jumbo stuffed sloth, doubling as my go-to living room throw pillow. Amidst a whirlwind of intellectual pursuits, I navigate the treacherous waters of AP Physics 1, where the fundamental forces of the universe relentlessly demand my attention; the tranquil, yet equally demanding expanse of an 11-page research report for AP Seminar; and the tempestuous Java assignments that challenge me within the hallowed halls of Columbia University. In these academic battlegrounds, I grapple with the weighty mantle of my overachiever identity, often teetering on the brink of exhaustion.

Yet, amidst the relentless pressure, I find solace in the transformative power of knowledge and the satisfaction that comes from conquering complex challenges. As I traverse this academic landscape, I am propelled forward by an unquenchable thirst for understanding, a passion to delve into the intricacies of science and literature, and a determination to emerge victorious in the face of adversity. The journey may be arduous, but with every hurdle surmounted, I grow stronger and more resilient, eagerly anticipating the next intellectual adventure that lies ahead. Nestled within the verdant embrace of the Arboretum, I find solace and respite upon the velvety expanse of Slothogus's comforting form. A gargantuan, albeit sluggish, creature, he embodies the tranquility that has long been a balm to my restless mind. As I sit perched atop his furry back, I allow the symphony of nature's whispers to lull my thoughts into quietude, momentarily silencing the cacophony that so often plagues me. From his stitched smile, I learn to take advantage of what little downtime I have. Embrace the serenity of the sloth-like Slothogus, as I immerse myself in the comforting world of Gilmore Girls or The Great British Bake Off, moments that serve as a balm for my intellectually overloaded mind. These shared viewing experiences with my family become more than just entertainment; they are sanctuaries where we find solace, laughter, and camaraderie amidst the whirlwind of academia. Maybe by next year, I will have broken 1:00 in the 100 fly, finished my Girl Scout Gold Award Project, and developed the next cutting-edge software to synthesize singing. Maybe, I’ll turn in my first capstone project for Intro to Computational Linguistics. And maybe, I’ll have added another pillow to the collection, so I can take the day off and have a lie in. I’ll sleep on it.
