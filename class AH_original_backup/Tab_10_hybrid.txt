Title: A Culinary Transformation: My Journey to a Plant-Based Lifestyle

Nourishing my body with sustenance became a lifelong quest, starting as a self-proclaimed anti-vegetable carboholic in a household where processed snacks reigned supreme. For years, my kitchen was ruled by animal products, outnumbering their plant-based counterparts.

My transformation began with a pivotal event - my mother's cancer diagnosis. In response to her 100% whole food plant-based diet regimen, I embraced this new eating philosophy, showing my unwavering support. Together, we delved into the world of health documentaries, such as "What the Health" and "Forks Over Knives," and immersed ourselves in books like "The China Study" and "How Not To Die," authored by the featured doctors. My fascination with nutritional science blossomed as I discovered how certain foods could combat cancer or boost metabolism.

Each new food unearthed a wealth of knowledge on the role diet plays in maintaining health. I found that sweet potatoes and brown rice could alleviate acne and heart disease, while combining leafy greens with citrus fruits enhanced iron absorption rates. The art of pairing foods to achieve the perfect macronutrient balance became my passion. Little did I know that beans and rice made a complete protein!

Embracing sustainability became as much a part of my journey as nutritional science. Adopting a plant-based lifestyle meant contributing less to the environmental impact caused by animal agriculture. With each square meter of land, a farmer could produce 200 kilograms of soybeans versus just 16 kilograms of beef. I minimized my ecological footprint, switching from plastic snack bags to reusable beeswax wraps and using a foldable straw as my preferred appliance. The earth is not just the recipient of nourishment; it deserves to be nourished too.

My passion for healthy living led me to co-lead the Northern Nevada PlantPure Pod, a group dedicated to championing the whole food plant-based lifestyle. We are currently working on a restaurant campaign aimed at convincing local eateries to offer a plant-based, oil-free menu option and become PlantPure certified. The revelation that many restaurants rely on oil in their cooking sparked my desire to open a plant-based, oil-free café, which I envision as an affordable oatmeal café inspired by my Instagram page, morning_mOATivations. While oatmeal may not be the trendiest superfood, I am determined to make it the Beyoncé of breakfast: sweet, sassy, and power-packed. This venture allows me to educate people about nutritional science through the palate.

I believe that hands-on experience is vital for understanding good food's essence. By using my taste buds as a textbook to learn which flavors complement each other and which ones clash, I am better equipped to educate others about the benefits of a plant-based lifestyle. We have been conditioned to associate delicious food with guilt; however, this need not be the case. The most exhilarating feeling is discovering a delectable dish and then uncovering its health benefits.

My transformation has resulted in increased energy levels. While my classmates complain of fatigue, I thrive due to my body finally receiving the appropriate macros, vitamins, and minerals it requires. This boosted energy has propelled me to excel in running, earning me my high school Cross Country team's Most Improved award. Although I remain a selective eater, my preferences have shifted significantly. Rather than a carboholic, I identify as a vegeholic, embracing a plant-based lifestyle with fervor and determination.
