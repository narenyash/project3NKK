In the labyrinth of my memory, my grandfather's words continue to reverberate like a powerful symphony, a testament to the sacrifices that charted our family's course. Born in the heart of a humble Nigerian village, my grandfather, ensnared by poverty, saw education as the sole beacon guiding him out of darkness. Against insurmountable odds, he navigated his way to Columbia University, transforming his life and establishing a firm foundation for generations yet unborn. Although he now resides in the annals of memory, his tales continue to guide my footsteps.

My father carried this torch forward, braving the deeply entrenched racism that plagued pre-Civil Rights America. He too sought solace within Columbia's hallowed halls, a sanctuary where his intellect could flourish despite the adversity he faced beyond its walls. With a wry smile, he would joke about fleeing from white women, a grim acknowledgement of the fear and paranoia that often shadowed Black men in those times. Yet, undeterred, he forged ahead, ascending to become a distinguished medical professional, proving that his father's sacrifices were not in vain.

Born on American soil, I have been blessed with opportunities my grandfather and father could scarcely fathom. However, their trials and tribulations are never far from my thoughts; they serve as the fuel that ignites my determination to carry forward the legacy they so valiantly built. Columbia represents more than just an institution for me; it is the bedrock of our family's aspirations, the crucible where our resilience was tempered and refined. Forged in the fires of perseverance, Columbia remains my one and only choice—a place where I find no alternative, as its legacy intertwines intricately with my own. To join the ranks of the third generation of Columbia graduates and medical professionals from my lineage is not merely an aspiration; it is a sacred duty that binds me to them.

Each examination and extracurricular activity I undertake represents a step forward on my journey, a testament to the miles my grandfather traversed on hands and knees, and the battles my father fought with fists as well as intellect. Columbia is not merely a dream for me; it is an inheritance, a destiny, and a solemn pledge I make to them.

I've made my decision.

Now it's your turn.
