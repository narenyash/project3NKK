Title: Pillowed Narratives: A Bedtime Tale of Growth, Passion, and Sloths

In the wee hours of my seventh birthday, I found myself in an uncharacteristic display of obstinacy, my face scrunched up as I clung to a mountain of pillows. The thought of spending the night away from my Stitch face pillow and my dolphin pillow pets was nothing short of horrifying. This overnight trip threatened my comforting and predictable nightly routine, a ritual I still cherish today, ten years later. As I flop onto my bed, I am met with the familiar embrace of my personally curated pillows, each one symbolizing a different facet of myself:

My chibi avocado pillow, green and round like the vegetable (fruit?) I once detested, now serves as a reminder of the importance of embracing new experiences. The first taste of guacamole at our favorite Mexican joint left me wrinkling my nose in disgust, but the avocado anecdote, as I call it now, brings a smile to my face as I remember the message my parents sent: never judge a book by its cover.

Next to the avocado, a ruffled teal pillow from Target stands tall atop its matching duvet. This purchase was the result of an artistic epiphany struck during middle school, inspired by an article in American Girl Magazine's spring spread. Design continues to fuel my creative flame, whether through coding or character art. Inspired by the "Coding Projects in Scratch" book, I ventured into block-based programming, designing graphics-based puzzles and platformers that laid the foundation for more advanced CS work in Python and Java. In Christopher Hart's "Master Guide to Drawing Anime," I find lessons in dynamic character composition and cell shading, skills I have applied to my own digital art.

Propped against the headboard is a My Melody pillow, a symbol of my embrace of the once-abhorrent cute. At thirteen, the thought of sleeping with a pink, fluffy bunny pillow (Hello Kitty-branded, no less!) filled me with dread. Now, My Melody represents my whimsical side, allowing me to unapologetically enjoy bubbly idol music or squeal over the turquoise, twin-tailed virtual popstar Hatsune Miku. As I tune synthesized singing vocals for my VOCALOID cover channel on YouTube, my inner geek emerges, piercing through my usually studious and composed exterior.

Lounging lazily beside my bed is the master pillow, Slothogus, a jumbo stuffed sloth that doubles as my go-to living room throw pillow. As my mind races between AP Physics 1, AP Seminar assignments, and debugging Java projects for my Columbia U class, I often find myself overwhelmed by my academic ambitions. In these moments of mental turmoil, I seek solace on Slothogus' plush shoulder. From his stitched smile, I learn the importance of taking a moment to relax and let go of my academic-induced anxieties. I take a deep breath, allowing Gilmore Girls or Great British Bake Off episodes to soothe my frazzled nerves alongside my family.

As I look towards the future, I envision breaking the 1:00 mark in the 100 fly, completing my Girl Scout Gold Award Project, and unveiling the next groundbreaking software for synthesized singing. I anticipate turning in my first capstone project for Intro to Computational Linguistics and, perhaps, adding another pillow to my collection - a well-deserved day off to indulge in some much-needed rest.

I'll sleep on it.
