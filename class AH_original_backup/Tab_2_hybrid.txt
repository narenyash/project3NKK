Title: The Vibrant Canvas: My Laptop as a Journey Map

My laptop, adorned with a kaleidoscope of stickers that decorate its surface, is my passport to the world – a testament to the diverse experiences, passions, and communities I've embraced. Each sticker serves as a memento, marking a stop on a journey that started long ago and continues to unfold. Let me guide you through this colorful itinerary:

"We <3 Design," nestled in the bottom left corner, is a symbol of my enduring love for art. As far back as I can remember, creativity has been an integral part of my life. Today, design serves not just as a means to brand and market, but as a platform to explore texture, perspective, and contrast – enabling me to hone my unique professional style.

A striking black and red rectangle in the bottom right corner bears the theme of the 2017 TEDxYouth@Austin event, "Common Threads." For years, I've been captivated by the street artists and musicians who inhabit the heart of Austin, living their lives unapologetically. This experience has broadened my horizons and fostered a deep appreciation for alternative lifestyles. TED provides me with an opportunity to share these diverse perspectives with other youth, fostering understanding and celebrating the rich cultural tapestry of our world.

A playful poop emoji graces the middle right section of my screen, a remnant of my younger brother's quirky communication style. When I receive his messages, hundreds of poops cascade across my screen, serving as a gentle reminder of the goofy side that he brings out in me. He has also taught me to remain calm and rational when overwhelmed – an essential skill that I carry with me in my personal and professional life. We may not have a typical sibling rivalry (though there are moments when I long for solitude), but we share a bond that transcends the ordinary – a friendship that is as strong as it is unique.

"Lol ur not Harry Styles," peels off the corner with the help of layers of tape, a relic from my seventh-grade infatuation with the charismatic heartthrob, Harry Styles. Beyond his boyband fame, Styles represents an artist and activist who uses his platform to effect positive change in society. His commitment to causes like the Time's Up Legal Defense Fund, Little Princess Trust, and LGBTQ+ equality has inspired me to become a more public advocate for social justice, rather than keeping my convictions confined to my own thoughts.

Nestled between the layers of stickers is "Catapult," the logo of a startup incubator where I launched my first company, Threading Twine. This experience taught me that business has the power to address fundamental human needs, such as economic empowerment and education – particularly for minorities. As I embark on my career, I aspire to be a corporate champion for women's rights, working towards institutional change and advocating for gender equality in high-level positions.

"Thank God it's Monday," hidden in the upper right corner, is one of my favorite stickers. It serves as a constant reminder that work should be a source of inspiration, not dread.

The Human Rights Campaign's horizontal yellow stripes, the "Kode with Klossy" emblem, and "The Team" from the Model G20 Economics Summit are just a few more examples of the diverse stickers adorning my laptop – each telling its own story, chronicling my evolution as an individual and as a global citizen.

When my computer eventually succumbs to time (I hope not for many years), it will be like the expiration of a passport. It will be difficult to part with these memories, but I'm certain that the next set of stickers will reflect the aspirations and adventures awaiting me in the future. They are the key to new paths, knowledge, and connections – a vibrant canvas that is always being painted with the strokes of life's ever-unfolding journey.
