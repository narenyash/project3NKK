Title: Moments Where Time Stands Still: A College Essay Example

Montage Essay, "Other/Advanced" Type

My time is a precious commodity, as cherished as my Scottish grandmother's hard-earned savings. I am meticulous in its allocation and apprehensive about squandering it. Priceless moments can convey affection and tip the balance between achieving ambitions or missing the opportunity to even begin – the stakes are high, for my academic pursuits, my show choir commitments, and my friendships all compete for a spot on my time schedule. Yet, there exist instances where the seconds cease to progress.

As I pull into our driveway under the cloak of night after a long day at school and rehearsals, I am greeted by the sight of Kona, my dog, leaping with joy upon my arrival. With an exuberant slide across the tiles, he rushes towards me as I open the door – a warm welcome that never fails to bring a smile to my face. In unison, we dash into my parents' bedroom, where they eagerly await my return. We convene on their bed, sharing stories about our lives, planning adventures, trading jokes, and gossiping – all while disregarding the ticking clock. These precious moments offer a respite in my otherwise hectic life.

Amidst laughter and camaraderie, my show choir members and I fill the rehearsal room with jokes and spontaneous movements that pass the time as if we were unaware of the hours slipping away. Immersed in our storytelling, we lose track of reality – a testament to the deep bond we share. Offstage, this sisterhood persists; my choir members become an extension of my family. I discovered early on that my passion for choreographing was not driven by recognition but rather by the desire to help sixty of my closest friends find their rhythm. In turn, they have helped me discover my voice.

Enveloped by the icy embrace of the ocean, I feel a sense of exhilaration as my scuba gear propels me beneath its surface. The rhythmic ebb and flow of the tide create an hypnotic symphony that grounds me in the present moment. As I venture deeper to explore vibrant marine life, I experience a harmonious dance with these creatures – free, synchronous, and unbounded by time. My fascination with marine biology led me to volunteer as an exhibit interpreter for the Aquarium of the Pacific, where I share my passion for the ocean. While rescuing animals from curious children and shielding children from accidental dips into tanks, I've formed lasting connections with visitors who share my love for the ocean and my dedication to its preservation.

A question echoes through my mind as I carry a distraught girl across campus in search of medical supplies – "Why are there no medical supplies available?" Her pain and fear mirrored my own, for I had experienced similar setbacks during performances. In that moment, the chaos of the show fades away, and I become singularly focused on alleviating her suffering. After locating the sports medicine training room, I tend to her injury with the care and attention it deserves. Since then, I've established a sports medicine program to provide support for the 500-strong choir program.

Saturday morning bagels shared with my family, backing up Barry Manilow on stage with my choir, swimming alongside sea turtles in the Pacific, bringing smiles to my teammates' faces despite their pain – these are the moments I cling to, the ones that shape my identity and define my aspirations. Time, for me, is not merely a countdown of seconds on a clock; it serves as a measure of what truly matters.
