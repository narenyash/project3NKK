Titled: Unveiling Life Lessons from Earthly Treasures: A Personal Journey through Rock Collecting

In my youth, I cultivated a peculiar fondness for the earth beneath my feet, meticulously seeking out intriguing stones concealed within nature's embrace - whether nestled under shrubbery, discarded in forgotten cupholders, or secreted away in the seemingly useless front pockets of women's jeans. My fascination with rocks persists to this day, not for their monetary worth or aesthetic appeal, but because they serve as vessels containing valuable life lessons.

My collection is a testament to memories rather than monetary value. One such memory resides in what I affectionately call my "dragon egg." As a child, my friends and I would gather fist-sized stones and imaginatively transform them into dragon eggs. We concocted magical potions from shampoo, daisies, and glitter to nourish our dragons, making the rocks shimmer under sunlight. Emotionally attached to this symbol of creativity, I chose to keep my dragon egg, reminding me never to lose that sparkle or innovative spirit. This essence of creativity manifests in various forms today. I immerse myself in Minhwa, traditional Korean watercolor painting, where my mother breathes life into Korean folktale characters and landscapes. My piano playing offers solace and joy, while my fashion sense reflects a kaleidoscope of styles ranging from Y2K to dark academia, even incorporating a touch of "Shrek-inspired" whimsy. These creative pursuits serve as essential sources of rejuvenation and reflection, fueling the passion that ignites other facets of my life.

The beauty of resilience is another lesson I've learned through my collection, exemplified by my affinity for collecting shards of cracked pavement. Much like my dedication to tennis and sports, both embody the essence of adaptability in the face of adversity. As I honed my skills on the tennis court, eventually securing a place on the varsity girls' team, I discovered that success emerges from perseverance and an ability to find beauty amidst hardship.

Sedimentary stones, composed of diverse clasts (or the rocky equivalent of crumbs), symbolize the deliberate design I've adopted in shaping my academic journey. My unquenchable thirst for knowledge propels me to delve into specialized courses like Auto and Human Anatomy, offering intellectual challenges while resonating with practical applications. The coursework does not merely serve as a means to excel on tests; rather, I ponder how to apply the knowledge gained in various educational and life contexts. For instance, APUSH didn't merely teach me history, but also fostered leadership skills that enabled my success in leading SciOly and HOSA. Like sedimentary rocks, I collect these "clasts," growing stronger, more resilient, and capable with each passing day.

One stone, retrieved from the bed of rocks on my patio, left a small gap that now houses an enigmatic tree. Over seven years and four months, I've devoted 2000 hours to studying trees, rocks, and rivers in search of identifying this elusive arboreal mystery. Dedicating 500 hours specifically to learning how to identify over 250 different trees, I remain undeterred by my inability to pinpoint the exact species. This conundrum serves as a humbling reminder that there is always more to discover, learn, and explore. Every glance at this tree (that quite literally "stumps" me) becomes a motivation to appreciate growth and continue striving, even amidst challenging circumstances.

In moments of contemplation, I find profound life lessons buried within the mundane rocks that surround me. These seemingly silent, unchanging guardians of time have served as my unexpected mentors. Each rock is a testament to the fact that wisdom and growth can be found in the most ordinary of things, inspiring me to continue my journey.

As I delve deeper into the earthy soil once more, I eagerly await the discovery of the next rock that will guide me forward on this enriching path.
