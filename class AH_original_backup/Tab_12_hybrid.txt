Title: A Linguistic Odyssey: Translation as a Journey of Empathy and Growth

In my early years, I was known as "miK ijniM," a name written from right to left that only I could comprehend, for I was a left-handed child with an unusual writing style. It wasn't until years of practice that I became ambidextrous, capable of deciphering my own cryptic script. This personal saga marked the beginning of my translation journey, a voyage that has since transformed my life in countless ways.

My translation abilities were first honed in the realm of mathematics, where I reinterpreted my Calculus teacher's explanations of L'hospital's rule into a powerful tool for solving complex problems. As I delved deeper into this subject, I discovered that teaching these concepts to others was not just a means of review but a vital step towards true comprehension. Initially, I shared my insights with friends as a way of reinforcing my own understanding. Today, I volunteer my time as both a Korean tutor for those enamored with Korean culture and a golf tutor for new teammates, using these opportunities to fortify my grasp of new concepts.

My translation talents extend beyond the realm of academia, serving as a "therapist" for friends and family alike. By decoding subtle hand gestures, facial expressions, and vocal inflections, I uncover the underlying emotions that often lie concealed beneath superficial words. In times of need, I put myself in their shoes, considering what emotional support I would want or require if I were in a similar situation. Through these acts of empathetic translation, I have become a more reliable and astute confidant, sister, and daughter.

However, my experiences, though rich, are not infinite. To expand my horizons and broaden my perspective, I've compiled a list of adventures that push me beyond my comfort zone. This catalog includes personal milestones such as traveling abroad solo, publishing my own book, and delivering a lecture before an audience. Though these aspirations may seem mere scribbles in the front pages of my diary, they have served as catalysts for vivid imaginations of myself achieving them. By enriching my experiences, I aim to become a fully empathetic therapist who can provide heartfelt advice grounded in a wealth of lived experience.

My translation skills found professional application in my work as an English to Korean letter translator for the non-profit organization Compassion. In this role, I serve as a communication bridge between generous benefactors and children living in developing countries. Hundreds of letters have passed through my hands, each requiring careful research into the cultural context and nuances of the recipient's language to ensure accurate translations. This experience has sparked an interest in learning additional languages, such as Spanish and Mandarin, as I believe that understanding diverse tongues is key to self-discovery. As I delve deeper into various linguistic worlds, I find myself evolving—my interactions with people change depending on the language I speak, allowing me to better understand myself and forge new friendships.

However, a career in translation is not my ultimate aspiration. Instead, I seek to become an ambulatory care clinical pharmacist, specializing in the management of patients afflicted by chronic diseases. In this role, I would employ my translation skills as a "therapist," responding effectively to patients' needs by empathically immersing myself in their situations. Furthermore, as a clinical pharmacist, I envision myself serving as a personal tutor who not only guides patients through the proper use of medication but also offers emotional support when it is most needed. My qualities as a "therapist" and a "tutor" have shaped me into a skilled translator, and I look forward to continuing this evolution as I embark on my career as a clinical pharmacist.

In essence, throughout my life, I have been, and will continue to be, a translator in one form or another—a journey that has led me through mathematics, language, and human emotion, enriching my understanding of the world and deepening my connections with others.
