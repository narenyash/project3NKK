Title: Empowering the Unseen: A Journey of Technological Innovation with a Conscience

Tab 14
Ngaitlangalbinus Lyngdoh's heartfelt blessing, penned by one of the countless daily wage workers in Meghalaya, India, resonated profoundly. It was more than just a message; it was a testament to the transformative power of an online resource—a social audit website I had the honor to create—that became a beacon of hope for the underprivileged.

In response to the Government's CRAWL and BOCW cash transfers during the COVID-19 lockdown, our research group at IIT Delhi and BITS Pilani, led by me, collaborated with TUR to create a platform where beneficiaries could voice their grievances. The website, available in Garo, Khasi, and English, featured an elastic search functionality, making it accessible to all. Our investigation revealed that a significant portion of street hawkers, vendors, and domestic workers did not receive the full or any compensation due. This project served as a poignant reminder of technology's potential to drive meaningful change and shaped my vision for a future in innovation with a conscience.

As I embark on my graduate studies, I am driven by the ambition to delve deeper into HCI and AI research, with a focus on using technology responsibly and contributing positively to society. I am particularly interested in responsible machine learning, prioritizing algorithmic fairness, interpretability, and personalization, alongside developing participatory systems that balance technical precision with ethical considerations. My ultimate goal is to become a professor specializing in HCI and AI, dedicating my career to ensuring fair access to resources for underprivileged classes.

My journey towards this goal began during my thesis at the University of Washington's ICTD Lab under Prof. Richard Anderson in Spring 2023. Here, I developed the Ekichabi Dashboard, which analyzed user behavior for an USSD application tailored for retrieving agricultural enterprise contact information in Tanzania. By identifying three distinct user behaviors and parameters such as most common modality, modality of failures, and user behavior, I was able to address shortcomings in the existing application and contribute to its enhancement.

Another significant project was at BITS Pilani, where I developed a 5-fold XLMRoberta model for a voice information retrieval system, integrating digital assistants to bridge India's agricultural digital literacy gap. Our survey confirmed the model's effectiveness and underscored the importance of localized solutions.

My interest in ML and AI was further cultivated through my experiences at Arizona State University's SURI (Summer Research Initiative) program, where I aimed to predict streamflow variability for climate change in the Logan River Watershed using an attention-based Conv-LSTM model. Validated against historical data, my model achieved an NSE of 0.90, surpassing the previous result of 0.84. This experience offered a glimpse into the rigorous yet rewarding life of a Ph.D. student and broadened my understanding of time series-based AI models.

Throughout my academic journey, I have faced and overcome various challenges. During my internship at the Indian Space Research Organization, I was tasked with refining a 30-meter Digital Elevation Model (DEM) to a super-resolution DEM using Lidar-based Photon elevation data and ML. This project required me to devise entirely new approaches to solve complex challenges, teaching me the art of discovery.

Another significant personal challenge I encountered was maintaining my academic focus during the emotional turmoil of losing three family members due to the COVID-19 pandemic. These experiences have prepared me for my Master's program at NYU, where I aim to refine my technical expertise and pursue cutting-edge research in HCI and AI.

NYU's commitment to research in HCI and AI, backed by world-class faculty and facilities, and its vibrant tech ecosystem, offers an ideal setting for my aspirations. The opportunity to collaborate with interdisciplinary institutes within NYU's culturally diverse campus will provide the perfect crucible for honing my skills and contributing to significant technological advancements with a meaningful societal impact.
