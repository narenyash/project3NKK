Title: Soaring Through Life: A Journey with Thomas Paige

In the realm of flight, all you need is a question. Allow me to guide you on a journey that soars from the origins of the universe to the intricacies of my personal growth, illuminating who I am – Thomas Paige.

The question echoing in your mind right now – Who is this enigmatic figure called Thomas Paige? – serves as a launchpad, propelling us through cosmic events back to the birth of our world. From the Big Bang, it swiftly carries us to the formation of our planet, the intricate dance of life and DNA, and the emergence of early human civilizations. We traverse time and space, delving into the fertile crescents of antiquity, the towering marble structures of Athens, the sacred temples of Jerusalem, and the New World's proclamation of equality for all men.

This celestial voyage takes us to my birth in the tumultuous aftermath of Hurricane Katrina, where I was cradled by my mother's light amidst the storm's darkness. We move through the pages of countless books that lined the edge of my twin bed, filled with tales of whimsical fairy dust and unforgiving truths that shaped me. We wade through the white rivers of Appalachia, where I found courage and strength amidst the treacherous rapids of Bayless' Boof. We taste the flavors of life at Kosher Cajun Deli, where I learned the value of hard work and selflessness. We revel in the vibrant cultures of Mardi Gras and Jazz Fest, immersing ourselves in a world that pulses with rhythm and resilience.

We attend the school on the Mesa, where my unique light was nurtured. We share dark nights in my brother's dorm room, where I understood the weighty responsibility of brotherhood. We gather around the round table of Company Burger, where I discovered that family is not solely defined by blood. We trek through the snow-capped peaks and valleys of Yosemite, feeling the awe-inspiring expanse of nature's grandeur.

As we delve deeper, we are granted a seat beside me on my flights – to witness the questions I grapple with and their far-reaching consequences. Here, we encounter my holy trinity: Who am I? Where do I belong? What should I strive for? Each query splinters into countless others: Why start another day? What defines humanity? What systems govern me? Whom do they serve? What needs to be altered? How can I best assist others?

We embark on excursions through the thoughts of philosophers like Plato and Machiavelli, explorers such as Portolá, and characters from media like Willie Stark, Aldous Huxley's Door of Perceptions, Pink Floyd, Andy Warhol's pop art, South Park, and The Impractical Jokers. Yet, this exploration extends beyond the realms of academia and art – it also encompasses my participation in the WRITE Program and Saint Joseph's Spanish mass.

These questions have transported me to the farthest corners of the globe, to fantastical realms of wonder and introspection, to revelations and awakenings, to heartache, laughter, and joy. They stir my creative spirit, broaden my human experience, deepen my understanding of my past and present, challenge my beliefs, and probe at the mysteries of my reality. I persistently ask them, for in doing so, I learn and grow.

*Bee buum* You are safely back on solid ground – or perhaps, just steps away from your desk. The question – Who is Thomas Paige? – soars through this journey, but offers no definitive answer. Only the towering history upon which I stand remains, along with a candid glimpse into the essence of another human being.

With eyes locked on yours, I declare: I am a seeker, and my life's purpose is to question, learn, and grow.
