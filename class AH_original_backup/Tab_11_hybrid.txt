Tab 11R
THE JOYFUL JOURNAL: A DIGITAL CHRONICLE OF LIFE'S MOMENTS
Montage Essay, "Essence Object" Type
The golden hues of a sunset casting a warm glow on a cherished friend and the comforting aroma of parmesan-laced spaghetti fill my senses - "14." The weight of looming assignments threatens to crush me as illness keeps me confined at home - "3." A photographic adventure through the heart of Seattle for a Spanish project unfolds before me - "15." For over two years, my Joyful Journal, a Google Sheet documenting numerical, descriptive, and visual representations of my happiness, has been my digital archive. It asks me to rate my level of joy on a scale of 1-20 and provide brief commentary on the day's events. However, its practical function is only part of its significance in my life.
A "14" etched on November 15, 2018, signified the commencement of the Lakeside Cooking Club, an initiative born out of a whimsical idea that has grown into a biweekly gathering of high school peers where we prepare delightful afternoon treats for one another. Months later, a "16" scribbled on February 27, 2019, marked the completion of a much-needed fence for the dilapidated soccer field in a small Colombian village. Days filled with mixing cement and transporting supplies culminated in the satisfaction of contributing to a community that had quickly won our hearts. The Joyful Journal is not just an outlet for my personal thoughts and emotions; it serves as a testament to the joy I find in sharing happiness with others.
If joy illuminates the path of my life, my family forms the intricate network of cities connected by those paths – each member a unique district, a unique story. In times of turmoil, whether preparing for a complex derivatives test or presenting my research at an international conference, I seek solace in my father's wisdom. Born amidst the bustling streets of Thiruvananthapuram, India, he teaches me to see beyond the clutter and recognize the hidden triumphs that lie hidden. In moments of self-doubt, I find refuge in my mother's reassuring presence. Her experiences living in her tranquil Japanese home help me prepare for my first high school dance or face the final match in a tennis tournament. When the numbers in my Joyful Journal dip low, my family is always there to guide me back towards "10."
The Joyful Journal also serves as a gauge of enthusiasm. At times, it buzzes with energy, like when I struck the last note on the piano for my competition-winning composition or when I persuaded a teacher to broadcast over the school speakers a holiday medley that my friend and I had recorded. Other times, the battery drains, leaving me disheartened by creative blockages, when not an ounce of inspiration sparks within. The Joyful Journal can be a trophy case, but it can also serve as a catalog of setbacks, struggles, and grueling challenges.
The journal began on an ordinary school day following a particularly puzzling physics test. The idea emerged spontaneously during lunch, and I shared it with two friends, inviting them to join me in this unique endeavor. We never expected the practice to last more than a couple of weeks or months, but now, after surpassing 700 days, we wonder if we'll ever call it quits. To this day, I ponder its significance in my life. Each new number I input serves as a reminder that each entry is not what defines me; rather, it is the interconnected line of data points that reflects who I am today. With every valley, I find the resolve to press on and with every mountain's peak, I recognize the valleys I've traversed to reach new heights. What adventures will the Joyful Journal lead me to next?
