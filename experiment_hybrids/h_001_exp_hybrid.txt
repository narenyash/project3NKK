The “My Pillows & Me” Example College Essay
“I can’t sleep without them!” my face scrunched up, clutching piles of cushions in an uncharacteristic moment of seven-year-old stubbornness. An overnight trip without my Stitch face pillow? My dolphin pillow pets? This was absolutely unacceptable, robbing me of the comfort and structure that was my nightly routine. Today, a decade has elapsed since that formative experience, and I find solace in the ritual of collapsing onto my meticulously assembled bed, enveloped by an assortment of pillows, each harboring a distinct facet of my personality: one cushion encases my fervent intellectual curiosity, another cradles my artistic flair, while yet another embraces my compassionate spirit. These pillows are not merely objects, but tangible representations of the multifaceted individual I have become as a result of my experiences and growth over the past ten years. Nestled beside me, my cherished chibi avocado pillow stands as a testament to a milestone achievement - my daring venture into the culinary world of guacamole. This souvenir, a celebratory gift from my parents, serves not only as a source of comfort but also as a reminder of the courage it took for me to step out of my gastronomic comfort zone. Green, mushy, yucky! I once wrinkled my nose in disgust at the outward appearance of this vegetable (fruit?): Upon concluding an afternoon of intellectual stimulation, my friends and I often indulge in a much-anticipated visit to our beloved local Mexican restaurant, where we savor every bite of their exquisite tableside guacamole. The intricate process of its preparation - from the precise chopping of ripe avocados to the delicate blending of spices - is a testament to the restaurant's commitment to authenticity and quality. Each spoonful offers not just a taste sensation, but a momentary escape that reminds me of the rich cultural heritage I seek to immerse myself in during my college years. In the present, as I gaze upon this avocado-hued pillow, the memory of the "avocado anecdote" brings forth both laughter and a poignant echo of the wisdom imparted by my parents. This episode serves as a powerful testament to their advice to always approach novel possibilities with an open mind, rather than dismissing them at first sight. Nestled beside the avocado, a plush ruffled pillow in a vibrant shade of teal, sourced from Target, rests comfortably atop its harmonious counterpart, a similarly textured and hued duvet cover. Initiated by a burst of creative energy, I made the decision to transform my bedroom during my middle school years. One item that served as both a catalyst and a tangible symbol of this transformation was a pillow I purchased, inspired by an aesthetically captivating article featured in the spring spread of American Girl Magazine. Design continues to fuel my burning creative spark, whether through computing or character art. Through the "Coding Projects in Scratch" text, I discovered a wealth of inspiration that propelled me into the realm of block-based programming. This introduction served as the catalyst for me to create my own series of graphics-based puzzles and platformers, thereby establishing a strong foundation for my future pursuits in computer science at a more advanced level with Python and Java. With a fervent dedication to honing my artistic abilities, I have immersed myself in the comprehensive teachings of Christopher Hart's "The Master Guide to Drawing Anime." Through rigorous study and practice, I have become adept at incorporating dynamic character composition and the intricate artistry of cell shading into my own digital art. These valuable skills have not only broadened my artistic repertoire but also fueled a newfound passion for the nuanced world of anime illustration. Propped against the headboard, a My Melody pillow cushions my head, a nod to my former aversion to all things “cute.” At age thirteen, the mere thought of being seen sleeping with a pink, fluffy, bunny pillow (Hello Kitty-branded, no less!) horrified me. In the realm of my personal interests, few entities embody my fervor and enthusiasm more distinctively than My Melody and Hatsune Miku. These vibrant characters serve as a testament to my embracement of an exuberant and whimsical side, evident in my unabashed enjoyment of bubblegum idol music and the infectious delight I express when immersed in the mesmerizing world of Hatsune Miku, her distinctive twin tails gracefully swaying in shades of turquoise. While tuning synthesized singing vocals for my VOCALOID cover channel on YouTube, my inner geek shines through my usually studious and composed exterior. Lounging lazily beside my bed sits the master pillow, Slothogus, the 3 foot tall, jumbo stuffed sloth, doubling as my go-to living room throw pillow. Nestled within the crucible of intellectual curiosity and rigorous academic pursuit, I frequently encounter moments of intense cognition as my mind navigates an intricate labyrinth of subjects. In AP Physics 1, I delve into the fundamental forces that govern our universe, constructing a deep understanding of these principles through hours of dedicated study. Simultaneously, I labor over an 11-page research report for AP Seminar, meticulously weaving together disparate ideas and perspectives to generate thoughtful insights on complex issues. Concurrently, my participation in a Columbia University class demands the debugging of Java assignments, each task serving as a testament to my commitment to mastering this versatile programming language. These academic endeavors, while demanding, have honed my ability to multitask effectively and maintain a high level of focus under pressure. Yet, the relentless pursuit of excellence often leaves me feeling overwhelmed, challenging me to strike a balance between my insatiable curiosity and the need for self-care. In an attempt to find solace amidst the cacophony of thoughts that often consumed me, I would momentarily seek refuge upon the comforting embrace of Slothogus' soft fur. From his stitched smile, I learn to take advantage of what little downtime I have. In the serene confines of my home, I find solace and respite from the relentless pressures of academia, channeling my thoughts into the comforting rhythms of Gilmore Girls and The Great British Bake Off. These television shows, shared with my beloved family, serve as a balm to soothe my overworked mind, offering moments of laughter, reflection, and camaraderie amidst the whirlwind of my scholarly pursuits. Maybe by next year, I will have broken 1:00 in the 100 fly, finished my Girl Scout Gold Award Project, and developed the next cutting-edge software to synthesize singing. Maybe, I’ll turn in my first capstone project for Intro to Computational Linguistics. And maybe, I’ll have added another pillow to the collection, so I can take the day off and have a lie in. I’ll sleep on it.
